About

About Antares Project

A new star is born, and is growing fast.

Antares is an SQL client that aims to become a useful and complete tool, especially for developers. It runs on Windows, Linux and macOS, ARM included, and works with MySQL/MariaDB, PostgreSQL, SQLite and Firebird SQL; more databases are on the way.

Give it a try and send us your feedback: it shapes what comes next.

Schema diagram

Simple, but complete

Features in the right places,
not hundreds of tiny buttons or submenus.

Antares SQL is driven by the idea of a full featured tool, fast and with a modern and intuitive UI: every feature is a few clicks away, productivity comes first.

Right-click a schema to open its diagram: tables and foreign keys at a glance, to drag around and zoom, with the relations of the selected table highlighted.

Query editor

Autocomplete or format your queries

Built-in autocomplete and database suggestions
speed up the writing of your queries.

The query editor speaks the SQL dialect of each database and suggests tables, columns and keywords while you type.

With the formatter (Ctrl + B) the queries you write become even more readable.

Import and export

Move your data around

Plain SQL dumps,
between any of your connections.

Export a whole schema, or only the tables you need, to a .sql file and import it on another connection in a few clicks, with MySQL/MariaDB, PostgreSQL, SQLite and Firebird SQL.

Antares also imports the dumps made by mysqldump, mariadb-dump, pg_dump and pgAdmin, so moving from another tool is easy.

Themes

Make it yours

Light or dark, high contrast or your favorite editor colors.

Install themes from the gallery right inside the app: each one restyles the whole interface together with the code editor. Want your own? Design it in the theme editor with a live preview, then share it in the gallery.

Principles

Free and open source

No paid content, subscriptions or ads. Antares will always be 100% free and open source.

Always evolving

Antares is a constantly developing project, and every update brings new features.

Community driven
